## Two-minute demonstration script

“I built this Power BI report using synthetic insurance operations data to show how I approach performance management.

I start with the Executive Overview. For June 2026, service level is 80.5% against an 85% target. Quality is also below target, while processing time and customer satisfaction meet their targets. Volume and backlog declined, so I would investigate why service level weakened before deciding on an action.

The Operational Performance page lets me compare teams, channels and transaction types. I can identify where service or quality gaps are concentrated and review the age of the backlog. I would use this to prioritize investigations with operational leaders.

The Customer and Quality Insights page adds satisfaction, first contact resolution and complaints. The aim is to improve speed while maintaining quality and customer outcomes. The scatter chart helps identify teams for further investigation; it does not prove that processing time causes satisfaction changes.

Technically, the report uses dimensional tables, reusable KPI measures, time comparisons, targets, navigation, slicers and a supporting team tooltip. My focus is to make the information usable for decisions and to define measures consistently.”
